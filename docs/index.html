Behavioral Compositionality of Sparse Autoencoder Representations

If the effects of individual SAE features are known, can their joint effect be predicted by summing them?

Haotian Ma, Ziye Mei, Frederic Sala University of Wisconsin–Madison
~20 s

Talk track

  1. SAE features are read one at a time, but explanations, circuits and steering use several of them together. This talk asks whether their effects add up when they are combined.
  2. There are five steps. We show the problem, define the measure, compare coordinates, look at feature pairs, and end with steering.

At layer 21 and the last token, the activation hx is split into the 16 most active SAE contributions δi=zidi and all other activation content hx0, which includes the reconstruction residual. Keeping a subset S gives

h˜x(S)=hx0+∑i∈Sδi

SAR measures what individual feature effects leave unexplained

SAE Additivity Residuals

Subsets are drawn by keeping each feature independently with probability 1/2. Let Πrg be the best approximation of g with terms of at most r features and V the variance of g. Then

SARr=‖g−Πrg‖μ2V,Δ2=SAR1−SAR2,ρ2=Δ2SAR1

Under this sampling SARr is exactly the normalized energy of interactions above order r, and it bounds the coalition ordering error of the best order r approximation.

With 16 real features there are 65,536 subsets. The paper samples 384, fits on 256 and reports SAR̂r=L̂r/(V̂+ϵ) on the other 128.

The toy uses all 16 subsets of four features, so its values are exact. Its behavior is g=A+0.5B+C+0.4D−AC+ABD.

Takeaways

SAE coordinates are comparatively compositional, but faithful joint explanations still need interactions

What these results do not show
  • A property of a whole SAE dictionary. The results depend on the selected features, the behavior and the intervention.
  • That pairs are always enough. Some higher order structure remains, and pairs did not help in every steering domain.
  • What the interacting features mean.
~30 s

Talk track

  1. Three points. Individual feature effects leave a residual everywhere we looked. SAE coordinates add up better than random rotations of the same subspace. Pairs recover most of what is missing, for explanations and for steering.
  2. These results depend on the selected features, the behavior and the intervention, so they are not a property of a whole SAE dictionary. Pairs do not remove all interaction, and we do not interpret what the features mean.
